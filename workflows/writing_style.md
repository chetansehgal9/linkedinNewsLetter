# Your Writing Style Guide

## What is this file and why does it matter?

This file teaches the AI your voice before it writes anything.

Think of it like hiring a ghostwriter on their first day. You wouldn't just say "write like me" — you'd show them your best work, explain what you hate, and describe who you're writing for. That's exactly what this file does for Claude.

**The more you fill in here, the less you'll need to edit drafts.**

You don't need any technical knowledge to fill this out. Just answer honestly. Skip any section that doesn't apply to you.

## SECTION 5 — What You NEVER Write

These are the guardrails. Claude will actively avoid anything on this list.

**Common things people want to avoid** (delete the ones that don't apply to you, add your own):

- Phrases like: "In today's fast-paced world...", "I'm excited to share...", "Honored to announce..."
- Ending with: "What do you think? Drop a comment below!" (too generic)
- Listicles that pad length: "5 things I learned about X"
- Corporate-speak: synergy, leverage, empower, disruptive, paradigm shift
- Starting a sentence with "I" too many times in a row
- Emojis scattered throughout the text
- Passive voice everywhere
